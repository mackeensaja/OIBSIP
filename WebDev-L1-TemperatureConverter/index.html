Temperature Conversion Formulas

Quick reference for the three temperature scales.

Celsius to Fahrenheit

(°C × 9/5) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius

(°F − 32) × 5/9

Celsius to Kelvin

°C + 273.15

Did You Know?

Absolute zero is the lowest possible temperature. It is equal to 0 Kelvin, −273.15°C or −459.67°F. Temperatures below this limit are not physically valid.
